MY STORY

Writing since I was seven

A booklover from an early age, I began writing stories at seven, and that turned into a lifelong dream of becoming a writer.

When I'm not reading a new book, jotting ideas in a notebook or pottering in my veggie garden under my cats' supervision, I'm on stage in amateur theatre or teaching English and French to high school students.
